# Prompt arena v1

Opt-in, server-authoritative, cartoon combat for 2–8 claimed people. Two fighters is a duel; three to eight is free-for-all. One lobby/match per office. Everyone starts at 100 HP and selects a public blade, bow or guardian loadout; cosmetic equipment has no combat effect. Prompts (1–1600 characters) and loadouts lock at countdown. Editing a lobby entry clears readiness. Only the host starts/cancels; all participants must be ready. Host departure transfers ownership. Rematches start an empty lobby, requiring fresh consent and tactics.

One server-selected model decides each living fighter's action concurrently against the SAME public snapshot plus only that fighter's private prompt and recent actions. No tools, opponents' prompts, free-form reasoning or invented actions. Strict JSON and engine validation are authoritative. Invalid actions/timeouts defend. Three consecutive turns without any valid model decisions abort without competitive credit.

The 12×12 arena uses Manhattan movement/range, obstacles blocking movement and straight-line attacks. A move travels at most two cells. Movement conflicts (including swaps) fail; attackers act from resolved positions. Defense halves incoming weapon damage; shields absorb remaining weapon damage. All attacks land simultaneously, including attacks from fighters eliminated that turn. Specials have a three-turn cooldown. Pickups require collect at the current tile; heal +25 HP, shield +20 (cap 30), boost +8 weapon damage for three turns. Knockout credit goes to the largest weapon-damage contributor that turn (seeded tie-break), never self/pressure damage. Shrinking safe bounds begin on turn 15, with escalating unblockable pressure damage. At 40 turns, a sole survivor wins; multiple survivors or simultaneous final elimination are a draw. Eliminations on the same turn share placement. Seeded spawns, pickup placement and tie-breaks allow replay from the same action stream.

Lifecycle: lobby → 3-second countdown → deciding (10-second hard deadline) → resolving (1.8-second animation interval) → next turn or finished/aborted. Browsers are not the match clock. Persist snapshots, private prompts, reservation and decision job IDs at turn boundaries. Recovered in-flight work defends rather than repeating paid calls. Stale callbacks cannot advance a different job/match. Spectators receive public snapshots and bounded combat events only. Compact completed matches retained: latest 20; aggregate leaderboard retained up to 1000 people. Prompts stay private unless their owner explicitly reveals the frozen prompt of a finished/aborted retained match.

Budget: reserve the worst-case number of calls (players × 40 turns) before countdown against a configurable daily call limit. Charge reservations conservatively, without refunds, including cancellation/recovery; no unbounded retries. Each request has bounded input/output and time. This is a call/token envelope, not a dollar guarantee: model pricing must be checked by the operator. Provider failures consume the reserved budget. Arena budgets are independent of avatar generation. Only one match may issue at most eight requests concurrently. Server credentials never reach browsers. Tactics must not include secrets; they are sent to the configured AI provider and stored server-side until their retained match expires.

Identity is the existing courtesy claim, not strong authentication: someone with the shared office password can impersonate connector names. Competitive results are for friendly office play, not adversarial public ranking.
